# HO51 — Down Names What A Transport Left

**Status:** Idea.

**What must be true.** Where `down` cleared the vault although a transport could not clear what it keeps
([HO50](README.md#ho50)), its protocol says which transport and project that was, in Porelo's own words, and that the
person dismisses the host on that homeserver if they have not.

## Why

Today the step's name alone says it happened: the lines callback writes a message on a failed line only, so the
transport's words in `porelo vault clear --json` reach nobody.

## To be checked

- Whether the lines callback should carry a message on an `ok` or `changed` line for one tagged task, or `down`
  writes its own last line.
- Whether the interface shows it: it reads the protocol's lines (porelo-frontend).
